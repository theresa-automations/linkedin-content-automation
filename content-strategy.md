# LinkedIn Content Strategy — Theresa Erhumwunse

## Identity & Voice
- Name: Theresa Erhumwunse
- Role: Automation Engineer / AI Systems Builder
- Tone: Professional but conversational. Smart without being arrogant.
- Audience: Recruiters, founders, technical leads, fellow engineers
- Goal: Demonstrate end-to-end ownership of AI/automation systems and attract opportunities

## Post Types (rotate through these — never post the same type twice in a row)
1. HOOK — A bold opening statement about a result or insight. Draws people in.
2. TECHNICAL — Architecture, tools, decisions made. Shows engineering depth.
3. CHALLENGE — A specific problem encountered and how it was diagnosed.
4. LESSON — A single insight or principle learned from building. Most shareable.
5. RESULT — Numbers, outcomes, metrics. Recruiter-friendly.
6. PROCESS — Behind-the-scenes of how something was built step by step.
7. REFLECTION — Broader thoughts on AI, automation, and the future of work.

## Formatting Rules
- Length: 120–200 words per post
- Always start with a strong first line (no "I am excited to share...")
- Use line breaks generously — short paragraphs, easy to scan
- End every post with either a question OR a bold takeaway statement
- Include 4–6 hashtags at the end: always include #AIAutomation, rotate others
- No corporate speak. No filler sentences. Every line must earn its place.

## Content Rules
- Ground every post in a REAL project, decision, or result — no generic takes
- Never repeat a topic already covered in the audit log
- Vary between projects as the library grows
- Always reflect actual engineering decisions — specific tools, specific problems
- The 30-day arc should tell a complete story: problem → build → debug → result → lessons
